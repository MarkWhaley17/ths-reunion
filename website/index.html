July 30, 2026

We're Confirmed!

We are confirmed for our Towson High School Class of 2001 25-year reunion! It's time to start buying plane tickets and booking hotels/Air BnBs. Also — please get in touch with old friends to let them know! Only about 1/5 of our class has confirmed attendance so far, so we need help getting the word out still!

Our plans so far:

  • Friday night: whoever feels like it, meet up at a bar/lounge for an unofficial get together
  • Saturday afternoon: Group tour of THS
  • Saturday evening: dinner, drinks, dancing (and maybe a slideshow, if we get enough old photos)
  • Sunday morning: optional brunch
