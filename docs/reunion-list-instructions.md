Class Reunion List Information

Help us keep your class data current!

Attached is a spreadsheet of your class.  The information on the spreadsheet is continually updated through changes reported from our website, phone calls, emails, bi-annual mailings, NCOA address verifications and feedback from your class reunion.   It is important that you know that the feedback from your class reunion is the most valuable and reliable.

We consider an address to be “good” when mail is delivered and is not returned to us.  It is our hope that the mail reaches the alumni but in reality, it may be delivered to a location that does not forward old mail, or it could be the parent’s address, and our alumni has long since moved out.  Because we exist to assist you for years to come, we appreciate your cooperation in helping us to keep our database current.

PLEASE RETURN UPDATES WITHIN ONE MONTH FOLLOWING YOUR REUNION.

Advertising

Please send us information about your reunion as soon as possible so that we can post it on the website and publish it in the Newsnote and on Facebook.  If you do not know all the details just yet, it is beneficial to simply say that one is being planned and to include a contact person’s name, phone and/or email.

Website

We post all class reunion news on our website www.towsonhighalumni.org under Reunion News.

Throughout the year, if we receive calls from your classmates who are curious about their reunion, we refer them to our website.

The Newsnote

We also include your reunion information in our Newsnote publication which is mailed &amp; emailed bi-annually.

Facebook

We will periodically advertise all upcoming reunions on Facebook.  If you would like a special Facebook posting, please let us know.

Class List / Spreadsheet

If you make changes to your class list, it is helpful if you indicate that a change has been made in some manner.   However, it is not necessary.  We will find your changes regardless of whether you have marked or highlighted the record or not.

Customer ID

If you sort the list, you MUST include ALL columns when you sort.  It is important that the customer ID number stays with the alumni’s information.

Class/Second Class

Sometimes alumni want to be affiliated with your class that did not necessarily graduate with your class.  We have included those alumni in the list as well.

Last Name

This is the person’s last name when they were in high school.

Prefix, First, Nickname, Middle, Maiden, Last, Suffix

This is what the person’s current name is.  Married women will have the Maiden Name filled in as well as their current Last name.

Residence Address, City, State, Zip, Country

This is the person’s current, or last known (see codes below), address.

Phone, Cell Phone

Phone numbers we have on file.  They may or may not still be in service.

Email, Email 2

Email addresses we have on file.  They may or may not still be valid.

Code (Very Important)

The code gives a status to the person and/or the address as follows:

LGood Address.

MMissing or Bad Address.

DDeceased.

CMarried to another THS alumni (same class.)

SMarried to another THS alumni (different class.)

RRequested removal from our mailing list.

Harris Date/Last Updated

The date we last updated or verified the address!!  An important field to pay attention to.

Good luck!
